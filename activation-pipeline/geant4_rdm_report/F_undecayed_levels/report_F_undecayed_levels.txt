Radioactive decay: 155 long-lived levels are never decayed correctly, and no
warning is given
==============================================================================

- Components: processes/hadronic/models/radioactive_decay
  (G4VRadioactiveDecay::LoadDecayTable, DecayIt, data RadioactiveDecay6.1.2
  and 6.1.3d) and particles (G4IonTable::GetIon, G4IonTable::FindIon,
  G4NuclideTable::GetIsotope)
- Geant4 version: 11.4.1 (datasets G4ENSDFSTATE3.0, RadioactiveDecay6.1.2 and
  6.1.3d, PhotonEvaporation6.1.2)
- Platform: macOS 15.8, Apple silicon (arm64)
- Severity: decay radiation is lost, or comes from another level with another
  lifetime; no warning

All numbers are for long-lived levels (1 s <= T1/2 < 10^15 s) and for
RadioactiveDecay6.1.3d, the release that fixes bug 2794. Where the 6.1.2
numbers differ, they are given in parentheses. Geant4 behaves the same with
both.

The levels are taken from ENSDFSTATE.dat of G4ENSDFSTATE3.0, the file that
G4NuclideTable reads and that README_RDM names as the source of the
half-lives. According to their History files, G4ENSDFSTATE3.0 and
RadioactiveDecay6.x were both generated from the ENSDF data of March 2024, so
the two files describe the same set of levels: 6493 of the 6552 P records of
6.1.3d match a level of ENSDFSTATE.dat.

Summary
-------

Geant4 decays a level correctly only if two lookups succeed:

1. Identity: G4IonTable::GetIon(Z, A, E, flb) creates the requested level.
2. Data: G4VRadioactiveDecay::LoadDecayTable finds the decay data of that
   level in RadioactiveDecay. The energy and the floating-level base (+X, +Y,
   ...) must both match.

These lookups fail, without a warning, for two sets of levels:

- No decay data (section 1): 139 levels (147). No P record in the library
  matches them.
- Levels never created (section 2): 99 levels. These are E = 0 levels. Some
  nuclides have more than one level at E = 0: a ground state plus an isomer
  whose excitation energy is unknown (0+X), or two states whose order is
  unknown (0+X, 0+Y). At E = 0, G4IonTable ignores the floating-level base and
  always returns the first E = 0 level listed in ENSDFSTATE.dat. A request for
  any other E = 0 level of the nuclide gets that first level, with its
  lifetime and decay data, which usually differ from those of the requested
  level.

83 levels (90) are in both sets, so 139 + 99 - 83 = 155 levels (156) are
affected. What Geant4 does with them:

       What Geant4 does                                       Result                                       Levels   Examples
    a  creates the level; no decay data                       killed at its decay time, no radiation       55 (56)  Ta178[0X] (2.36 h), Cm252 (2.0 d), Bi198 (10.3 min)
    b  creates the level; no decay data                       de-excited by IT instead of its beta- decay  1        Pm152[150X] (13.8 min)
    c  creates another level instead, one with decay data     wrong lifetime and radiation                 87 (86)  Sb120[0X] (5.76 d) decays as Sb120 (15.9 min); Np240[0X] (7.22 min) as Np240 (1.03 h)
    d  creates another level instead, one without decay data  no radiation                                 12 (13)  Ta178[0Y] (9.31 min) becomes Ta178[0X]; Bk248[0X] (23.7 h) becomes Bk248[0Z] (9 y)

In total, 67 levels (69) (a + d) emit no radiation at all, and 88 (87) (b + c)
emit the radiation of the wrong level or decay mode.

Case d includes the worst example, the W178 chain. W178 (T1/2 21.6 d) decays
100 % by EC to Ta178[0Y] (1+, 9.31 min). Ta178[0Y] has decay data, but Geant4
cannot create it; it creates Ta178[0X] (7-, 2.36 h) instead, which has no
decay data. All Ta178 radiation of the W178 chain is lost: the Hf X-rays, the
93 keV gamma and the beta+ branch.

Three paths in Geant4 request levels that cannot be created (cases c and d):
- radioactive decay: a daughter is created with the floating-level base given
  in the decay record (e.g. G4ECDecay.cc:63). Eight decays of parents with
  T1/2 >= 1 s feed such a level (section 2);
- hadronic de-excitation: G4PhotonEvaporation keeps the floating index of the
  level where the cascade ends (G4PhotonEvaporation.cc:585-586), and
  G4ExcitationHandler passes it to GetIon (G4ExcitationHandler.cc:554-561). A
  spallation or capture residue that ends on such an isomer is created as the
  first E = 0 level;
- direct user requests, e.g. /gun/ion 73 178 0 0 Y.

The fix of bug 2794 in 6.1.3d added decay data for six of these isomers
(Tb154[0X], Tb154[0Y], Ir186[0X], Sb128[0X], Np240[0X], Tc102[0X]). Geant4
cannot use them, because it never creates these levels (section 2).

Attached:
- check_F_undecayed_levels.py: a standard-library Python script. It lists (1)
  the levels without decay data, (2) the E = 0 levels that cannot be created,
  (3) the decay records that feed them, and (4) the classification a-d of the
  table above. It can also write an rdecay01 macro for the levels of list 1
  and classify the rdecay01 output;
- check_F_undecayed_levels_6.1.3d.out: its output on RadioactiveDecay6.1.3d,
  including the classification of the rdecay01 run;
- check_F_undecayed_levels.out: the same on 6.1.2;
- rdecay01_F_undecayed_levels.mac: a short macro for the unmodified example
  extended/radioactivedecay/rdecay01;
- rdecay01_F_all_levels.mac: the macro for the 139 levels of list 1 (6.1.3d),
  written by the script.

Floating levels in ENSDF
------------------------

A level energy is measured through the transitions that link the level to the
ground state. When no transition links a group of levels to the rest of the
scheme, ENSDF gives their energies relative to an unknown base: E+X, and Y, Z,
U, V, W... for further independent groups (ENSDF manual, field E).
G4ENSDFSTATE3.0 and RadioactiveDecay store only the number as the energy and
the letter in a separate column. So Ta178 0+X and Ta178 0+Y both have E = 0,
and only the letter tells them apart. NUBASE2020 estimates the Ta178 1+ level
at 100 +- 50 keV (from systematics) and flags the order of the two levels as
uncertain.

How to reproduce
----------------

    source <geant4-install>/bin/geant4.sh
    python3 check_F_undecayed_levels.py <RadioactiveDecay6.1.3d dir>    # default: 6.1.2 from $GEANT4_DATA_DIR
    export G4RADIOACTIVEDATA=<RadioactiveDecay6.1.3d dir>
    cd <rdecay01 build dir>
    ./rdecay01 rdecay01_F_undecayed_levels.mac                           # the cases below

    python3 check_F_undecayed_levels.py <RadioactiveDecay6.1.3d dir> --macro=rdecay01_F_all_levels.mac
    ./rdecay01 rdecay01_F_all_levels.mac > all_levels.log

decays and then as two full chains. The long macro decays 200 nuclei of each

1. Levels without decay data: 139 (147)
---------------------------------------

For an E = 0 level with no matching P record, LoadDecayTable returns an empty
decay table, and DecayIt kills the nucleus at its decay time without
secondaries (the "has no decays" branch). For an excited level with no P
record, LoadDecayTable instead adds an IT channel with branching 1
(G4VRadioactiveDecay.cc:877-880). There is no warning in either case, although
the level has a finite ENSDFSTATE lifetime.

Each of the 139 levels was created with /gun/ion Z A 0 E flb in rdecay01:
- 55 (56) are created as requested and emit nothing in any decay (case a).
- 83 (90) are never created: they are E = 0 levels of section 2, and Geant4
  creates another level instead (cases c and d).
- 1 is created and de-excited by IT (case b): Pm152[150X] (T1/2 13.8 min). It
  is an excited level with no P record, so it is de-excited through the
  PhotonEvaporation levels (gammas, Pm152 at 44.45, 25.02 and 16.03 keV) to
  the Pm152 ground state, which then decays with its own 4.1 min half-life.
  NUBASE2020 gives this level beta- 100 % (IT not established).

------------------------------------------

In ENSDFSTATE.dat, among the levels that G4NuclideTable loads:
- 161 nuclides have more than one E = 0 level. For 136 of them the first is
  the fixed ground state, for 25 a floating level. Four nuclides, Ta178,
  Pr134, Re172 and Pm136, have no fixed ground state at all.
- The 172 other E = 0 levels are never created. 99 of them have T1/2 >= 1 s:

The hadronic path cannot be counted from the data files. It applies to every
level of this section on which photon evaporation can end, e.g. Sb120[0X]
produced on Sn and Sb, or the Re182 and Ir186 isomers produced in spallation
of heavy targets.

Cause
-----

Section 1 (no decay data). For a level with a finite ENSDFSTATE lifetime and
no matching P record, LoadDecayTable returns an empty decay table if E = 0,
and DecayIt then kills the nucleus without secondaries (case a). If E > 0, it
adds an IT channel (G4VRadioactiveDecay.cc:877-880), whatever the real decay
mode of the level (case b). Neither issues a warning.

Section 2 (never created). At E = 0 the floating-level base is dropped in
three places:
1. G4NuclideTable::GetIsotope (G4NuclideTable.cc:118) accepts a level if flb
   == level->GetFloatLevelBase() || E == 0.0. So at E = 0 it returns the first
   level of the multimap, i.e. the first in ENSDFSTATE.dat.
2. G4IonTable::GetIon (G4IonTable.cc:483-490): when no ion exists yet, it
   takes the floating base of that level (flb1) and creates the ion with it
   (CreateIon(Z, A, E, flb1), line 500).
3. G4IonTable::FindIon (G4IonTable.cc:644-648) has a "rerun search on ground
   level without check on floating": at E = 0 it returns the first existing
   ion with E = 0, whatever its floating base.

In addition, all E = 0 levels of a nuclide get the same PDG encoding.
G4NuclideTable raises the isomer level only for E > 0 (if (ionE > 0) ++iLevel,
G4NuclideTable.cc:211), and CreateIon sets lvl = 0 when Eex == 0
(G4IonTable.cc:277). The encoding computed at line 287 is therefore the same
for all of them.

LoadDecayTable, by contrast, requires the floating base of the P record to
match that of the ion (G4VRadioactiveDecay.cc:611-618). So the decay data
written for a level that is never created are never used, and the level
created in its place may have no data at all (case d).

Effect in an application
------------------------

In our activation study for a high-energy astrophysics CubeSat experiment
(5-700 MeV protons on a satellite mass model, Geant4 11.4.1), W178 is produced
in the tungsten collimators. Ta178 (1+) is in secular equilibrium with it:
0.345 Bq out of 35.7 Bq of payload activity after one year in orbit. All of
its radiation is lost. We reran these decays with a private copy of z73.a178
in which the P 0 +Y block is relabelled +X
(/process/had/rdm/setRadioactiveDecayFile). The missing radiation is a small
but sizable fraction of the overall background.

Possible fixes
--------------

- In G4NuclideTable::GetIsotope and G4IonTable::FindIon, ignore the floating
  base at E = 0 only when the request has none (flb == no_Float). A request
  with a letter should get the level with that letter.
- Give each E = 0 floating level its own isomer level (and PDG encoding), as
  for E > 0 levels.
- Add decay data for the levels of section 1. For Ta178[0X], the
  RadioactiveDecay5.6 block could be restored.
- Issue a warning (or a JustWarning G4Exception, once per nuclide):
  - in LoadDecayTable or DecayIt, when an unstable nucleus with a finite
    ENSDFSTATE lifetime has no decay channel;
  - in GetIon, when the level returned has a floating base other than the one
    requested.
